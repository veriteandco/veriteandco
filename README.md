# Hi, I'm Mel

I build AI workflows and tools for small businesses through my studio, [VÉRITÉ](https://veriteandco.com/). I also work on AI adoption in institutional finance. I am most interested in AI deployment and implementation: getting these tools to work in real organizations, with the right guardrails, so people actually use them.

## What I'm sharing here

- **[website-qa-scan](https://github.com/veriteandco/website-qa-scan):** a console snippet that catches design drift on AI-built websites (fonts, colors, sizes, missing alt text, blurry images, broken links) against a design-system allowlist.
- **[inquiry-desk-demo](https://github.com/veriteandco/inquiry-desk-demo):** a clickable back-office concept for a small events business, with inquiries ranked by fit, follow-ups, calendar, quotes and reply templates. Sample data only.
- **[small-business-ai-playbook](https://github.com/veriteandco/small-business-ai-playbook):** the process I follow when bringing AI into a small business, from backups to measurement to guardrails.
